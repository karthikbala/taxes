# Twenty-county local verification cost gates

No new spending is authorized by this implementation. Preserve current ledger balances, reservations, provider receipts and historical cleanup outcomes. Existing per-county production override scope must not expand automatically to the twenty-county trial.

## Proposed bounded stages, pending approval

1. Direct official-source discovery and local parser work: no provider spending. Use small explicit county batches, maximum two concurrent requests/jobs and one navigation per host. Stop after two non-advancing diagnosis cycles and retain the failure.
2. Access feasibility: at most one managed capture for each of the twelve historically challenged counties, 120 seconds each. Proposal ceiling **12 captures / $1.92 accounting reserve**, using a proposed conservative $0.16 planning envelope per capture (twice the existing four-credit/$0.08 reservation at the application's $0.02-per-credit conversion). This is an internal ceiling, not a new vendor quote or subscription price. Verify current entitlement/billing and reconcile the ledger before requesting approval or dispatching anything.
3. Acceptance: select 240 real properties, then calculate paid-dependent cases from actual successful recovery routes. Approval must name counties, cases, capture ceilings, total cost, timeout and existing ledger baseline. A 240-capture/$38.40 accounting envelope is only an upper-bound planning illustration at the same reserve; it is not approval and cannot cover additional certificate/deed queries without separately itemized limits.
4. Feeds, accounts, OCR licenses, subscriptions and county records fees: obtain actual terms and quotes for the identified dependency. No price is assumed; no trial/license or request is activated by code.

Provider-reported credits and internal dollar conversions must remain distinct. A previous paid success or available balance does not authorize another test. Unknown capture/cleanup outcomes retain their reservation and require reconciliation before repeated work. Budget shortfall is BUDGET_PENDING, not a source omission. No automated live probes run from unit tests or CI.
